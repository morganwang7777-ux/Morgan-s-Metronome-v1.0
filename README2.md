# Mechanical Enclosure

The mechanical enclosure for this project was designed by **Jordan Lin**.

Jordan created a custom **SolidWorks enclosure** around the metronome PCB and supporting plate hardware. The enclosure was designed to securely package the electronics while maintaining access to the PCB and allowing the board to be removed when required.

## Enclosure Features

- Custom tapered SolidWorks casing
- **100 mm × 90 mm** PCB mounting cavity
- Separate **71 mm × 55 mm** secondary plate cavity
- Wiring clearance and pass-through features
- **M3 screw** pivot system
- Two movable PCB retaining rails
- Mechanical rail stops
- SolidWorks assembly testing to verify rail movement and PCB accessibility

## Design Revision

The original retention design used a single rail across the PCB.

During assembly testing, this rail was found to obstruct access to the PCB controls. The design was therefore revised to use separate **top and bottom retaining rails**.

This allows the PCB to remain securely mounted while keeping its controls accessible and making the board easier to remove when required.
